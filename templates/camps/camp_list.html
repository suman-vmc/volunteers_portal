Types of Environmental Camps

We offer a variety of camp types to address different environmental needs:

Reforestation Camps

Participate in tree planting and forest restoration to combat deforestation and create wildlife habitats.

  • Tree planting
  • Seedling nursery management
  • Forest monitoring

Coastal & Marine Camps

Help protect marine ecosystems through beach cleanups, coral restoration, and marine conservation efforts.

  • Beach cleanups
  • Coral reef monitoring
  • Marine wildlife surveys

Wildlife Conservation

Support wildlife conservation through habitat restoration, population monitoring, and protection activities.

  • Wildlife surveys
  • Habitat restoration
  • Anti-poaching patrols

Waste Management

Work on waste reduction, recycling initiatives, and cleanup campaigns in urban and natural areas.

  • Waste collection
  • Recycling programs
  • Community education

Sustainable Agriculture

Learn and practice sustainable farming methods to promote food security and environmental stewardship.

  • Organic farming
  • Permaculture design
  • Community gardens

Environmental Education

Assist in educational programs that raise awareness about environmental issues and solutions.

  • School outreach
  • Nature interpretation
  • Workshop facilitation
