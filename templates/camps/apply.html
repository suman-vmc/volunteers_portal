Application Process

  1. Submit your application
  2. Our team reviews your application
  3. You receive an email with the decision
  4. If approved, you'll receive camp details and preparation information
  5. Join us for the camp!

Terms and Conditions
Volunteer Agreement

By applying to participate in an Eco Volunteers camp, you agree to the following terms:

1. Participation

You commit to actively participate in all scheduled activities to the best of your ability. If you are unable to participate in certain activities due to physical limitations or other concerns, please indicate this in your application.

2. Code of Conduct

You agree to:

  • Respect the environment and wildlife
  • Follow safety guidelines and instructions from camp leaders
  • Be respectful towards other volunteers, staff, and local communities
  • Refrain from any form of discrimination or harassment
  • Not consume alcohol or drugs during camp activities
3. Health and Safety

You acknowledge that participating in environmental conservation activities may involve certain risks. You agree to provide accurate health information and notify camp leaders of any health concerns that may arise during the camp.

4. Media Release

You grant Eco Volunteers permission to use photographs, videos, or other media featuring you during the camp for promotional and educational purposes.

5. Cancellation Policy

If you need to cancel your participation, please notify us as soon as possible so we can offer your spot to another volunteer.
