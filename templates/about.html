About Eco Volunteers

Learn about our mission, values, and the positive impact we're making on the environment.

Our Story

Eco Volunteers was founded in 2015 by a group of passionate environmentalists who saw the need for a coordinated approach to local and global environmental challenges.

What started as a small group of dedicated individuals organizing weekend cleanup events has grown into a thriving community of volunteers committed to making a positive environmental impact.

Today, we organize dozens of environmental camps each year, focusing on conservation, reforestation, biodiversity protection, and environmental education.

Our Mission

Our mission is to protect and restore the natural environment through community-based volunteer projects and educational initiatives.

We believe that collective action is key to addressing today's environmental challenges. By bringing together passionate individuals and providing meaningful volunteer opportunities, we can create tangible positive impacts in our environment.

Our programs aim to:

  • Restore degraded ecosystems and habitats
  • Protect endangered species and biodiversity
  • Reduce waste and pollution
  • Promote environmental awareness and education
  • Foster a connection between people and nature

Our Values

Environmental Stewardship

We are committed to protecting and enhancing our natural environment for present and future generations.

Community Engagement

We believe in the power of communities working together to create meaningful environmental change.

Education

We're dedicated to increasing environmental awareness and knowledge through experiential learning.

Inclusivity

We welcome volunteers from all backgrounds and believe that diversity strengthens our environmental efforts.
